Natal IQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [PUBLISHER / COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Natal IQ mobile application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

Natal IQ is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Content, symptom-checker results and replies from our AI companion "Aanya" are for general wellness and educational purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health or your baby's health. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number immediately.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To personalise content to your pregnancy week (for example weekly tips and lessons).
  • To operate the community features and show your posts and comments to other members.
  • To generate replies from the Aanya AI companion.
  • To schedule local reminders and notifications on your device.
  • To keep the App safe, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your health information for advertising or marketing.

3. Service providers we share data with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore) – hosts account, profile and community data. Firebase privacy information.
  • Groq, Inc. – processes the text of messages you send to Aanya, along with the recent conversation, so it can generate a reply. No account identifiers are sent with these messages. Groq privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts – the App may download fonts from Google's servers, which receive your device's IP address as part of the request.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or others.

4. Community content is public within the App

Anything you post in a community, including your display name and pregnancy week, can be seen by other Natal IQ users. Please do not share sensitive personal details such as your phone number, address or medical records in posts or comments.

5. Data security

Data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, which encrypts data at rest. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Account, profile and community data is kept while your account is active.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time. Follow the steps at [ACCOUNT_DELETION_URL] or email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email address registered to your account. We delete your account, profile, posts, comments and likes within 30 days, unless we must keep some data longer to meet a legal obligation.
  • Data stored only on your device (chats, journal, tracker, reminders) is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.
