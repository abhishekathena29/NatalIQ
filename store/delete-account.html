Natal IQ is published by [PUBLISHER / COMPANY NAME]. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app, go to More → Delete account. Or
  2. Email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my Natal IQ account", from the email address you signed up with.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email address and user ID)
  • Your profile: name, pregnancy week, first-pregnancy status
  • Your community posts, comments, likes and memberships

Chats with Aanya, your journal, tracker entries and reminders are stored only on your phone. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Timeline

Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request. We keep no data afterwards, except where the law requires us to.
